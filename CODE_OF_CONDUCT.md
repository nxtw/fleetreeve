# Code of conduct

This project wants to be a place where people can work together without
being made to feel unwelcome.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient; assume good intent; criticise
ideas and code, not people; accept that others have different experience
and constraints than you.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults or demeaning remarks; sexual
language or attention; publishing someone's private information; any
conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

This applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other space where the project is represented.

If you experience or see behaviour that goes against this, contact the
maintainers privately through the address on the maintainers' GitHub
profiles. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions, and may exclude
people from the project temporarily or permanently for behaviour they
consider harmful.

This text is adapted from the ideas of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
